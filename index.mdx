## Get Started in 4 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at [app.ats-platform.com](https://app.ats-platform.com) and verify your email address. An administrator account is created automatically for your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Pipeline">
    Set up your hiring stages, candidate statuses, and team member roles from the **Settings** panel before you post any jobs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post Your First Job">
    Navigate to **Jobs → New Job** and fill in the role details, requirements, and publishing channels. Your job goes live instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track and Hire">
    Review incoming applications on the **Candidates** board, move them through your pipeline, schedule interviews, and issue offer letters — all in one place.
  </Step>
</Steps>
