The ATS Platform is a full-featured, cloud-based Applicant Tracking System built for recruiters, hiring managers, and HR administrators who need a single, reliable place to manage every stage of the hiring process. From the moment you publish a job posting to the day you send an offer letter, the ATS Platform gives your team real-time visibility, structured workflows, and the automation tools needed to move candidates through your pipeline without losing context or momentum. Whether you're a two-person startup scaling your first team or an enterprise HR department managing hundreds of open roles simultaneously, the ATS Platform adapts to the way your organization hires.

## Key Capabilities

The ATS Platform covers the full recruiting lifecycle through six tightly integrated capability areas. Each area is designed to work seamlessly with the others, so your data flows from job creation all the way through to a hired candidate without manual re-entry or disconnected spreadsheets.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Job Posting & Management" icon="briefcase" href="/manual/job-management">
    Create, publish, and manage job listings across your careers page and external boards. Control visibility, set requisition details, and track every posting's performance from a single view.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Candidate Tracking" icon="users" href="/manual/candidate-management">
    Maintain a complete profile for every applicant — résumés, notes, tags, activity history, and communication logs — so your team always has the full picture before making a decision.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Interview Scheduling" icon="calendar" href="/manual/interview-scheduling">
    Coordinate interviews across your panel with calendar sync for Google and Microsoft 365, self-scheduling links for candidates, and structured interview templates to keep evaluations consistent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Offers & Hiring" icon="file-contract" href="/manual/offers-and-hiring">
    Generate, send, and track offer letters with built-in e-signature support and PDF export. Manage approvals, capture accepted offers, and trigger onboarding steps automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reporting & Analytics" icon="chart-bar" href="/manual/reporting-analytics">
    Measure time-to-hire, source effectiveness, funnel conversion, and pipeline health with a real-time reporting dashboard. Export data for deeper analysis or share live reports with stakeholders.
  </Card>
  <Card title="REST API & Webhooks" icon="code" href="/api/overview">
    Integrate the ATS Platform with your HRIS, onboarding tools, or internal systems using the REST API v2. Subscribe to webhook events to trigger workflows the moment something changes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## User Roles

The ATS Platform uses a role-based access model to ensure that each person on your team sees and can act on exactly what they need — nothing more and nothing less. Roles are assigned per organization and can be customized at the team level.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="HR Admin" icon="shield-halved" href="/concepts/user-roles">
    Full access to all platform settings, user management, billing, integrations, and every open role. HR Admins configure the platform and own organization-wide policies.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recruiter" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/concepts/user-roles">
    Creates and manages job postings, owns the candidate pipeline, schedules interviews, and collaborates with hiring managers. Recruiters drive the day-to-day hiring workflow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hiring Manager" icon="user-tie" href="/concepts/user-roles">
    Reviews candidates assigned to their roles, submits scorecards after interviews, approves offers, and monitors pipeline progress for their open positions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Interviewer" icon="comments" href="/concepts/user-roles">
    Participates in scheduled interviews, submits structured feedback through scorecards, and views only the candidate profiles they have been explicitly invited to evaluate.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For a detailed breakdown of permissions per role, see the [User Roles concept page](/concepts/user-roles).

## Platform Versioning

The ATS Platform follows **semantic versioning** (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH). A major version increment signals breaking changes — such as API authentication header changes or removed endpoints — while minor versions introduce new features in a backward-compatible way. Patch versions contain bug fixes and security updates.

<Note>
  The current stable release is **v2.1.0**. API v1 is deprecated and scheduled for removal in Q4 2025. If you are still using API v1, review the [upgrade notes in the Release Notes](/release-notes) and migrate to API v2 as soon as possible.
</Note>

You can find the full version history, including changelogs, migration guides, and breaking-change notices, on the [Release Notes](/release-notes) page. Subscribe to the release notes RSS feed or check the in-app notification center to be alerted whenever a new version ships.
