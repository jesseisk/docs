The ATS Platform is a modern, cloud-native Applicant Tracking System designed to help your team manage every stage of the hiring process — from writing a job description to sending an offer letter. Whether you're a solo recruiter filling a handful of roles or an enterprise HR team managing hundreds of open positions, the platform scales to fit your workflow, keeps your candidate data organized, and gives you the visibility you need to make faster, better hiring decisions.

## The Three Phases of Hiring

Every successful hire follows three broad phases. The ATS Platform is purpose-built to support all three seamlessly, so nothing slips through the cracks.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Source" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Attract and capture candidates through job board integrations, your branded careers page, and manual sourcing. All applicants flow into a single, unified inbox so you never lose track of a lead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Evaluate" icon="clipboard-check">
    Move candidates through structured pipeline stages, conduct scored interviews, and collaborate with hiring managers — all in one place. Consistent scorecards and activity logs keep your team aligned.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hire" icon="handshake">
    Generate offer letters, collect legally binding e-signatures, and trigger onboarding workflows the moment a candidate accepts. The finish line is just as smooth as the starting line.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Multi-Tenant Data Isolation

Your organization's data is completely private and isolated from every other tenant on the platform. The ATS Platform uses strict multi-tenant architecture: your jobs, candidates, applications, and settings are stored in a dedicated logical partition and are never accessible to — or shared with — any other organization. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and access within your organization is governed by role-based permissions you control.

<Note>
  If your organization requires a dedicated infrastructure deployment (single-tenant), contact your account representative to discuss enterprise hosting options.
</Note>
