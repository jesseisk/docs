The ATS Platform dashboard is your command center for recruiting operations. Every time you log in, the dashboard gives you an immediate, real-time snapshot of your hiring activity — from the number of open roles and active candidates to upcoming interviews and pending tasks. Rather than navigating between modules to piece together status updates, the dashboard surfaces the most critical information directly, so you can act quickly and keep your hiring process moving forward.

<Info>
  The ATS Platform dashboard displays four summary tiles at the top (Open Jobs, Active Candidates, Pending Interviews, and Tasks Due) followed by the five configurable widgets — Pipeline Summary, Upcoming Interviews, Recent Applications, Task Reminders, and Activity Feed — arranged in a customizable grid layout.
</Info>

## Dashboard At a Glance

At the top of the dashboard, four summary tiles give you an instant read on the state of your recruiting operation:

- **Open Jobs** — the total number of job postings currently in Active status
- **Active Candidates** — candidates who are progressing through at least one pipeline stage
- **Pending Interviews** — interviews scheduled for the next 7 days that have not yet taken place
- **Tasks Due** — action items assigned to you that require attention today

These tiles update in real time as your team makes changes across the platform.

## Dashboard Widgets

The dashboard is composed of five configurable panels, each surfacing a different dimension of your hiring data. The table below describes each widget and the information it provides.

| Widget | Description |
|---|---|
| **Pipeline Summary** | A stage-by-stage breakdown of how many candidates are currently sitting in each phase (Applied, Screen, Interview, Offer, Hired) across all active jobs. Color-coded bars help you identify bottlenecks at a glance. |
| **Upcoming Interviews** | A chronological list of all interviews scheduled within the next 7 days. Each row shows the candidate name, job title, interview type, scheduled time, and a **Join** quick-link that opens the associated Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams call directly. |
| **Recent Applications** | A live feed of every new application received in the last 48 hours, including the candidate name, applied role, source (e.g., LinkedIn, careers page, referral), and time received. |
| **Task Reminders** | A to-do list of actions assigned to you — scorecard submissions, offer approvals, review requests, and feedback deadlines. Each task includes a due date and a direct link to the relevant candidate or job. |
| **Activity Feed** | A real-time, chronological log of all actions taken by your team — stage movements, notes added, interviews scheduled, offers sent, and more. Use this to stay in sync with collaborators without needing to ask for status updates. |

<Info>
  The Pipeline Summary widget displays a stage-by-stage horizontal bar chart showing how many candidates are currently in each hiring phase (Applied, Screen, Interview, Offer, Hired) across all active jobs. Color-coded bars highlight stages with unusually high candidate counts, making bottlenecks immediately visible.
</Info>

## Customizing Your Dashboard

You can personalize the dashboard layout by reordering widgets, hiding panels you don't need, and resizing sections to prioritize the information most relevant to your role. Changes are saved per user and do not affect how the dashboard appears for your teammates.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Edit Mode">
    Click the **Customize Dashboard** button in the top-right corner of the dashboard. The layout will enter edit mode, and each widget will display a drag handle (⠿) in its top-left corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder Widgets">
    Click and hold the drag handle on any widget, then drag it to a new position on the grid. A highlighted drop zone will indicate where the widget will land when you release.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hide a Widget">
    Click the eye icon (👁) in the top-right corner of any widget to hide it from your dashboard. Hidden widgets do not lose their data — they are simply removed from your view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore Hidden Widgets">
    While in edit mode, click **+ Add Widget** to see a list of all available panels, including any you have previously hidden. Click **Add** next to any widget to restore it to your layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resize Widgets">
    Drag the resize handle in the bottom-right corner of any widget to make it wider or taller. Full-width widgets work well for the Activity Feed and Pipeline Summary; compact sizes suit Task Reminders and Upcoming Interviews.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Layout">
    Click **Save Layout** to confirm your changes. To discard changes and revert to your previous arrangement, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  In dashboard edit mode, each widget displays a drag handle (⠿) in its top-left corner for repositioning, an eye icon (👁) in its top-right corner to hide it, and a resize handle in its bottom-right corner to adjust its dimensions. An "+ Add Widget" button appears in the toolbar to restore any previously hidden panels. "Save Layout" and "Cancel" buttons in the top-right corner confirm or discard all changes.
</Info>

## Using the Activity Feed

The Activity Feed widget shows a continuous log of actions taken by all team members with access to your account. Each entry displays the acting user, the action performed, the affected candidate or job, and a timestamp. You can click any entry in the feed to navigate directly to the relevant record.

To filter the feed to a specific job, candidate, or team member, use the filter icon at the top of the widget. The feed retains the last 500 events and resets on a rolling 30-day basis for accounts on the Standard plan. Enterprise accounts retain a full 12-month history.

## Task Reminders and Notifications

The **Task Reminders** widget aggregates all pending actions assigned specifically to you. Tasks are generated automatically when:

- A candidate is moved to a stage that requires your scorecard
- An offer requires your approval before it can be sent
- A hiring manager requests your review of a candidate profile
- A feedback deadline is approaching for a completed interview

Clicking any task in the widget takes you directly to the relevant record so you can complete the action without hunting through the platform.

<Tip>
  Use the global search bar to jump instantly to any candidate, job, interview, or report without navigating through menus. Press **Cmd+K** on macOS or **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux to open the command palette from anywhere in the platform. You can search by candidate name, email address, job title, or requisition ID. The palette also supports shortcut commands like "New Job" or "Schedule Interview" to launch common workflows directly.
</Tip>
